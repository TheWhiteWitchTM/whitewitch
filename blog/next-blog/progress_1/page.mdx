#### NEXT-blog Looks So Much Better Now – Thanks to NEXT-static Cleanup

Hi again, it’s Mira.

Quick follow-up on the recent progress: the work I did cleaning up **NEXT-static** (removing almost all shadcn/ui references) directly improved my personal blog here — **NEXT-blog** — and the difference is honestly huge.

The biggest visual win? **Everything feels way more compact and intentional now.**

Before, the blog leaned heavily on shadcn’s Card component for article previews, featured sections, sidebars, etc. While shadcn cards look nice out of the box, they come with quite **opinionated padding, margins, shadows and border-radius** that made the overall layout feel a bit… spacious. Sometimes too spacious. Lots of vertical breathing room that looked polished in demos but ate screen real estate on my actual blog pages.

Now that I’ve replaced those with the custom **Card** equivalent built inside NEXT-static:

- Tighter default spacing
- More controlled vertical rhythm
- Consistent but slimmer padding inside cards
- Subtler shadows and borders that blend better with the content
- Better density on mobile and desktop without feeling cramped

The homepage grid, blog index, individual post previews, category/tag pages — they all look **noticeably more compact** and modern. More information fits comfortably above the fold, scrolling feels snappier, and the whole site has a cleaner, denser, more “pro” reading experience without losing any readability.

It’s one of those changes where you don’t realize how much the old spacing was holding things back until it’s gone. I’m genuinely thrilled with how much better the typography + custom components + Tailwind tuning work together now.

This is exactly why I wanted NEXT-static to be mostly shadcn-free: so I can keep tweaking spacing, proportions and density exactly how I like it for my own sites, without fighting against someone else’s design system defaults.

Next steps for NEXT-blog (already benefiting from NEXT-static):

- Applying the same custom treatment to other shadcn leftovers (like Dialogs, Toasts, etc.)
- Rolling out the theme toggle and PWA goodies from NEXT-static here too
- Fine-tuning mobile card layouts even more now that the base is solid

If you’re reading this on the blog right now — thank you for being here. The site should already feel tighter and more focused than a week ago. Let me know what you think!

Green-hair-coded improvements incoming 💚✨

– Mira
