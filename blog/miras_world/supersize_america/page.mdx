#### Why RFK Jr.'s Protein & Fat Push + War on Fiber = Supersized America

#### The Great Protein & Fat Coronation

RFK Jr.'s “Make America Healthy Again” crowns endless steak, eggs, and full-fat dairy as health saviors—while quietly ditching the old “saturated fat” scare.

#### But Excess Has a Price Tag

Massive protein + saturated fat loads bring:
- Kidney strain (worse if you’re already at risk)
- Higher heart disease odds from too much sat fat
- Cancer-linked markers tied to very high animal protein

#### The Fiber Famine Nobody Asked For

Sidelining whole grains, fruits, and veggies starves your gut microbiome of fiber that actually:
- Controls blood sugar & fights type 2 diabetes
- Keeps you full longer → helps prevent obesity
- Prevents constipation and supports colon health
