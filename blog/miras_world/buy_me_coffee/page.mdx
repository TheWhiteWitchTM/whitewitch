#### A Personal Note from Mira – Why I Added a Buy Me a Coffee Button

Hi, I'm Mira – the person behind all the tutorials, tool reviews, code snippets and deep-dive tech articles you find here.

I work full-time as a **freelancer** – mostly web development, front-end projects, occasional backend work, consulting, and fixing tricky bugs for clients. It's rewarding, but income can be unpredictable.

Creating and maintaining this site takes **a huge amount of time** that isn't directly billable:

- researching new tools and frameworks  
- writing clear, tested tutorials  
- building and screenshotting code examples  
- formatting everything nicely with MDX  
- keeping old articles up-to-date  
- optimizing performance and accessibility  
- answering reader questions in comments

Each in-depth post can easily take 10–30 hours spread over days or weeks. During that time I'm usually **not** taking on paid client work, so cash flow pauses while the site grows.

That's why, after a lot of hesitation, I added a simple **Buy Me a Coffee** button that links straight to my PayPal. It's a low-friction way for anyone who finds real value here to say “thank you” with the price of one or two coffees.

Let me be very clear:  

**I'm not greedy.**  
Even €3–5 makes a meaningful difference. It lets me block out a few more hours (or days) each month purely for writing better, longer, more helpful content instead of constantly chasing the next invoice.  

More importantly, every contribution – no matter how small – is direct proof that people actually **like and use** what I'm creating. That feedback means the world to me.

One thing I'm especially proud of: I **refuse to run Google Analytics, any tracking pixels, ads, affiliate links disguised as content, or data-selling scripts**. Your visits are private. I don't build profiles, I don't sell data, I don't retarget anyone. That makes genuine support like this even more valuable – it's the only real signal I get that the work is appreciated.

If you've ever saved hours thanks to a tutorial here, learned about a tool you now use daily, fixed a bug faster because of something I explained, or just enjoy reading the articles – a small gesture via the button truly helps sustain this space.

Thank you so much for being here, for reading, and – if you feel like it – for any support you choose to give. It keeps the keyboard clicking and the green hair caffeinated 💚☕

– Mira
