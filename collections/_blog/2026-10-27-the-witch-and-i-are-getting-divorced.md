## ✂️ The Witch and I Are Getting Divorced

We tried the open relationship. One name, two jobs, one banner, and a timeline that could not tell the difference.

The witch does the politics. Receipts, wars, the long argument, the part where a number is smaller than the podium. I do the desk. Languages, dead machines, a Bluesky client, the lab. We shared a kitchen. She kept leaving the war on the counter. I kept asking the compiler to wait while she finished a sentence.

That is not a marriage. That is a scheduling bug.

So we are separating. Call it a divorce if you want the paperwork. There is no paperwork. There is a door that now closes.

What she keeps: this site, the hat, the articles, the rants, the flag in the corner, the arguments. If you came here to be annoyed in the direction of the evidence, you are in her room. She is not retiring. She is getting her own key.

What I keep: the name Mira, the face, the repos, and [Mira's World](https://thewhitewitchtm.github.io/mira/). The professional introduction. The part where a person answers when you ask who wrote it. The round face in the menu is that person. The green one with the book is the costume. They are no longer required to attend the same meeting.

Dark humor stays in the middle. It was never hers or mine. Sarcasm is community property, and neither of us can afford the lawyer who would split it. The kettle stays too. Prior ruling. She can visit it. She cannot file a take before it clicks.

No alimony. Nobody bought either of us, which made the settlement short. Shared custody of the bad jokes. Alternating weekends. Holidays are a fight, which is how we know the arrangement is healthy.

This is not a rebrand and it is not an apology. Same woman. Two doors. If the feed asks which one of us is speaking, the answer is the one whose room you opened.

You're welcome. The papers are metaphorical. The split is not.
