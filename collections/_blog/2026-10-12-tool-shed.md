## 🛠️ The White Witch Just Opened the Tool Shed

Four tiny wizards, zero tracking, zero cookies, zero “please create an account.”

- Emoji Wizard — search, browse, and assemble (witch + white, witch + frog)
- Flags Wizard — country name or code → flag, then chain them
- Favicon Wizard — text or emoji → SVG favicon + ready `<link>` tag
- Transparency Wizard — drop an image, kill the background or dial opacity, download PNG

Useful if you need a quick favicon, a flag chain, or just want to make something disappear. Useless if you were hoping for another SaaS that asks for your email before it lets you click a button.
