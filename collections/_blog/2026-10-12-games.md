Got so bored and annoyed with the nonstop political insanity flooding my zone that I rage-quit the timeline and started porting 70s and 80s games.

Turns out carefully resurrecting a 50-year-old codebase that still smells like punch cards is still less exhausting than watching adults argue in public. The universe is now slightly more cursed. You’re welcome.

### Currently playable
- [Galaxy Hunt](/games/galaxy-hunt/) — retro terminal space combat
- [Rogue](/games/rogue/) — ~200-line JavaScript dungeon crawler
- [Pong](/games/pong/) — the first game I ever played (on a cheap knockoff)
- [Snake](/games/snake/) — classic, no tracking cookies

Is any of it perfect? No.  
Does it still need polish? Yes.  
Am I having an absolute blast making browser versions with zero tracking, zero cookies, and zero databases? Absolutely.

The original Rogue had thousands of lines. This one has about 200. The original Galaxy Hunt was a terminal game with two colors (black and green). I kept the green vibe and swapped letters for emoji. No 3D card required.

Play them. Argue with the code, not the caption.
