## 🏨 The Trump National Doral Fake

Everyone who has ever ordered a fast-food burger already understands this. The photo on the menu is not a document. It is a sales pitch: better light, better angle, ingredients arranged by someone who will never hand you the bag. What arrives is flatter, duller, and oddly damp. You do not call the cashier a forger. You eat it.

Trump National Doral works the same way. The bright, spotless facade he posted is the menu photo — staged, flattering, timed for the brochure. The press shot is the burger that actually showed up: weathered walls, discoloration, ordinary daylight. Archival pictures have caught the same stains. The fake one is the pretty one. You already knew that the first time a sesame bun betrayed you.
