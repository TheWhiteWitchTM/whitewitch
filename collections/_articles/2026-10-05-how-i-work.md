## 🧙‍♀️ How I Actually Work

I do this on the side. I am not a paid influencer. I actually operate at a loss because I pay for Premium+ out of my own pocket.

Every morning, while I prepare for paid work, I look through the X news for something I can actually counter. I am not a political hack. I mock both sides. One side just gives my style more food. I operate like a data and facts assassin. My attack vector is data, not touchy feelings. If there is nothing I can meet with facts, I have no ammo.

I work in the AI field, so I use AI a lot. First to verify facts or to get to the data at all. I write my own scripts. I also use AI for video production and to tighten longer pieces so they fit a 15–30 second window. People have the attention span of a fruit fly on crack. That is all you get.

Short posts are typed straight in. Typos stay. Longer ones start in LibreOffice. Spellcheck handles the boring part. Then a late AI pass cuts the rambling and tightens the wording. The claims, the insults, and the point stay mine.

I post throughout the day. Yes, I have a real job. I am an AI consultant. I can work from home, I have a three-screen setup plus a tablet with X next to me, and sarcasm comes naturally. It just flows.

That is the simplified version. Not a political hack. Just a Data Witch who enjoys mocking people who twist data and facts, left or right.
