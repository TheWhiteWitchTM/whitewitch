## 😡 A Child Was Stabbed to Death at School

On 6 October 2026 a 15-year-old entered a middle school in Istanbul wearing a uniform and stabbed eight children between the ages of 10 and 13. One of them, 10-year-old Eslem Sare Bulut, died. The others were injured. The attacker’s parents were detained. [](grok_render_citation_card_json={"cardIds":["10b8ba"]})

That is the event. A teenager walked into a school, pulled a knife, and started stabbing other children. One of those children is dead. The school was supposed to be a place where that does not happen. It happened anyway. In the middle of the day. In front of other students. [](grok_render_citation_card_json={"cardIds":["364621"]})

The precise ages are not the interesting part. A 15-year-old with a knife and a 10-year-old who did not survive the attack are both children. The system that is supposed to keep kids from being murdered by other kids inside a school building failed in public. Officials called it heinous. That does not change the outcome.

If the response to a child being stabbed to death at school is still to argue about the numbers, then the problem is not the numbers. The problem is that this has become something people treat as debatable instead of obviously broken.
