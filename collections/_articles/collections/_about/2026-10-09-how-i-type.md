## ⌨️ Why I Write Fast (and Why the Typos Happen)

I have used keyboards since I was a tiny witch. I type blind. I do not look at the keys. That gets me roughly 70 words per minute, closer to 80 if I am not also watching the screen. That is also when the famous typos and grammar quirks appear.

Not looking has another advantage. I can hammer thoughts straight from mind to screen with no distraction. For longer pieces I then run a local AI pass to clean up. On short posts I do not bother.

I used to write for computer magazines and contributed to a couple of tech books. I have also written and delivered conference talks. Words just flow. On X I do not have to turn the personality down the way I do for paid work. That makes everything faster.

No filters. Same person. Just less overhead.
