## 😡 A Child Was Stabbed to Death at School

On 6 October 2026 a 15-year-old entered a middle school in Istanbul wearing a uniform and stabbed eight children between the ages of 10 and 13. One of them, 10-year-old Eslem Sare Bulut, died. The others were injured. The attacker’s parents were detained.

That is the event. A teenager walked into a school, pulled a knife, and started stabbing other children. One of those children is dead. The school was supposed to be a place where that does not happen.

No soft landing. No “but her feelings.” No timeline argument about root causes that magically erases the body. The facts are narrow and ugly: she got inside, she had a knife, she used it on kids, one of them did not survive. Argue with that if you want. The rest is commentary.
