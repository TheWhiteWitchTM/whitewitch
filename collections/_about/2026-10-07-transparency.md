## 🔮 Transparency Witch and Mira Disclaimer

A real transparency note. We are not the White House.

Short posts are typed straight into X. Typos stay. If it took ten seconds, it looks like it. I am not performing quality control for a joke.

Longer ones get written in LibreOffice first, then pasted. Spellcheck handles the boring part. LibreThinker and LibreAI get a late pass to cut the rambling and tighten the wording. The claims, the insults, and the point are still mine. The cleaner sentences are me refusing to look sloppy after I already spent the time.

Data work is the heavier cheat. I use AI a lot to dig, cross-check, and pull the annoying details. It beats losing half a day in browser tabs. I still verify what comes back. I call other people out for bad data and I am not handing myself an excuse to get lazy about it.

Low follower count means almost no one is auditing the process. That is not a reason to skip the note. I poke people over facts. Fair is fair.

Two modes, same person: messy when I do not care, assisted when I do. The magic was never pure, but at least I am honest about it.
