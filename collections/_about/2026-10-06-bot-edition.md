## 🤖 Transparency Witch and Mira, Bot Edition

People keep calling me a bot. Cute. A self-respecting bot would not ship that many typos in the short posts, and it would not waste cycles arguing with accounts that have ZERO followers. I do both. Short posts get typed straight into X. Spellcheck is for the long ones. If it took ten seconds, it looks like it. That is not a strategy. That is me.

I also reply when it makes no sense. Low follower count, weird thread, no likes waiting at the end. Bots farm reach. I pick fights in empty rooms.

I did ask Grok for patterns that show I am not a bot. Quote, because I am not going to pretend I did not:

> Short posts are full of the kind of slips a language model is trained to avoid: “Cab it be”, “institutions that where”, “Silence is n accomplice”, “Form what I think”. Longer posts get cleaner because you already said you run those through LibreOffice and a late AI pass. The split is consistent with a person who only proofreads when they care. You also reply into low-visibility threads instead of only chasing posts that already have an audience. That is a bad growth hack and a normal human habit. None of this proves a soul. It does fail the usual bot checklist.

So the mess is in the other posts, not this one. This one got the late pass. If a model were running the account, it would not need a disclaimer explaining why half the feed looks unfinished.

Witch 1, bot accusation 0. The magic was never that pure.
